Research

I study volcanic processes on Earth and across the Solar System, from active eruptions in Iceland to lava plains on Mars, Venus, the Moon, and Io. My group studies eruptions such as the 2014–2015 Holuhraun and 2021–2023 Fagradalsfjall events in Iceland and supports mission development through field-based planetary analog research, remote sensing, and numerical modeling.

Key Themes

Geologic surface processes: lava emplacement, magma–water interactions, volcano-tectonics, aeolian processes, megaflooding, and impact cratering.

Astrobiology: microbial colonization of volcanic terrains, biosignature detection, and habitability.

Geoinformatics: remote sensing, information theory, Bayesian inference, machine learning, and computer vision.

Exploration technology: unoccupied aircraft systems (UAS), light detection and ranging (LiDAR), and radar.
